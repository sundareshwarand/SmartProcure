# SMARTPROCURE
## Intelligent Farmer Procurement & Queue Management System

### Problem
Farmers often face long waiting times, lack of information regarding procurement schedules, congestion at procurement centres, and uncertainty about procurement and payment status.

### Solution
SmartProcure is a digital procurement management platform that connects farmers, procurement-centre operators, district officers and administrators through one integrated system.

The system allows farmers to book procurement slots, receive digital tokens, monitor their queue position, track procurement progress and receive payment information.

Operators can manage queues, perform quality inspection, digital weighing, amount calculation, procurement completion and payment processing.

Officers can monitor procurement centres and district-level activity, while administrators manage users, centres and audit information.

### Key Features

- Farmer registration and authentication
- Digital slot booking
- Digital queue/token management
- Live queue updates
- Procurement-centre monitoring
- Quality inspection
- Digital weighing

- Grievance management

### Expected Impact

SmartProcure reduces unnecessary waiting, improves queue visibility, provides transparency in procurement and payment, and gives procurement-centre staff a structured digital workflow.

The platform also creates centralized operational visibility for officers and administrators.
